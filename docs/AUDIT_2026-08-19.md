# Classifier Audit — Consolidated Roadmap (excluding training-data work)

Eight dimensions, 8 adversarial verification passes. Refuted findings dropped; corrected evidence used throughout. Where several dimensions independently hit the same thing, it is flagged — that convergence is the strongest priority signal in this document.

**The single most important conclusion:** the repo already contains the evidence that the current optimisation target is null, and it has not been propagated. `results/new_testset/corrected_testset_results.json` — the one evaluation in this project with fixed non-test-tuned thresholds, 10k bootstrap CIs and McNemar — records `champion_warm_ner0` F1=0.8743 / AUC=0.918 against `template_trained` (plain BiomedBERT on v7 templates) F1=0.8746 / AUC=0.9205, **McNemar χ²=0.093, p=0.7604**. And `results/all_models_eval_499_test_set.md:56` states outright: *"Champion (full_typed_a05_ner2) ranks 22nd by AUC — it was specifically optimised for EP-relax distribution."* Meanwhile CLAUDE.md and MEMORY.md still say "Best single model, beats ensemble by +0.011, hard ceiling 0.868". Roughly a year of architecture work has been steered by a 100-row difference the project's own broadest evaluation already contradicts.

## A. Correctness and validity — these undermine current claims

### A1. The champion does not beat the plain template-trained baseline, and the repo knows it
*Hit independently by eval-methodology and architecture-training.*

**Claim.** "Multi-task single model EXCEEDS ensemble by +0.011 F1" and "0.868 is the hard ceiling" are unsupported.
**Evidence.** `results/new_testset/corrected_testset_results.json`: champion 0.8743 vs `template_trained` 0.8746, McNemar p=0.7604, CIs [0.843,0.902] vs [0.844,0.903]. `results/all_models_eval_499_test_set.md:9-45,56`: champion 22nd of 30 by AUC (0.869); `transformer_BiomedBERT_cv_regularized` #1 (0.928), ensemble #2 (0.925). On EP-relax at n=100: ΔF1 champion−ensemble = +0.0108, 95% CI [−0.026, +0.052], **McNemar p = 1.00** (they disagree on 5 of 100 sentences). AUPRC inverts the ranking outright: ensemble 0.8619 > cv_reg 0.8433 > **champion 0.7969**.
**Change.** Replace the claims in `CLAUDE.md` and `MEMORY.md` with the `corrected_testset_results.json` numbers plus their McNemar p. Retire "0.868 hard ceiling". State in the paper that the top ~15 multitask configs and the template baseline are statistically tied.
**Impact.** Very high — this is the difference between a defensible thesis chapter and one an examiner dismantles. **Effort:** hours.

## B. High-leverage improvements

### B1. Calibrate — the model is trained at T=2 and read at T=1, and the fix is free
*Root cause found only in verification. Hit by calibration-decision and architecture-training.*

`experiments/multitask/train.py:35,53,55` train against `softmax(logits/2)`, but **all 17 inference sites** read `softmax(logits)` at T=1 (`evaluate.py:71`, `tier2_triple_extractor.py:166`, `api/fastapi_multitask.py:104`, `prelabel_curation_queue.py:55`, `eval_corrected_testset.py:48`, `bootstrap_ci.py:90`, …). Measured consequence: EP-relax **ECE = 0.1559**, Brier 0.1409; the [0.9,1.0] bin has n=41, mean confidence 0.991, **actual accuracy 0.780**; 81/100 sentences sit in the two extreme bins. `src/utils/outcome_codes.py:83` prints "confidence: 99%" for that population.

**Dividing logits by the recorded T=2** drops EP ECE 0.1559 → **0.1043** and 500-set 0.1831 → **0.1395**, F1 unchanged (monotone, zero label flips), and moves the argmax threshold to **0.28 — exactly the value train.py's own validation sweep produced.** No held-out split, no fitting. A residual fitted temperature (T≈3.3-4.8) buys a bit more.

Also persist it: `experiments/multitask/model.py:111-116` writes `multitask_config.json` with only `encoder_name`/`ner_scheme`/`alpha`/`ner_labels` — the checkpoint has lost the information needed to un-sharpen its own output.

**Impact:** high (methods-section credibility + every downstream consumer of `cls_prob`). **Effort:** hours.

### B2. Replace all fitted thresholds with one closed-form prior-shift rule stored with the checkpoint
*Hit by 4 dimensions; the single best measured lever in the audit.*

For `full_typed_a05_ner2` alone there are four thresholds in the repo — 0.28 (val, `train_summary.json`, read by nobody), 0.13 (EP test-swept, running in production at `tier2_triple_extractor.py:56`), 0.090 (`eval_new_experiments.py:42`), 0.25 (`evaluate.py` default) — plus 0.360 belonging to a different checkpoint, plus 0.15/0.25/0.50 elsewhere.

Closed form: `τ = sigmoid(−[logit(π_target) − logit(π_train)])` with π_train = 4,243/50,041 = **8.48%**. Measured:

| set | π | closed-form τ | F1 at τ | oracle F1 | val-derived τ=0.28 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EP-relax | 0.48 | 0.0912 | 0.8519 | 0.8679 | 0.8627 |
| 500-row | 0.562 | 0.0673 | **0.8505** | 0.8494 | 0.8203 |

**On the 500-row set the closed form lands *on* the oracle optimum and beats the shipped validation threshold by +0.030 F1.** It generalises across a different prior, needs no fitting, and makes cross-model comparison meaningful because every model gets the same rule.

**Change.** Record `train_pos_rate`, `temperature`, `threshold`, `data_sha256` and `metrics` in `multitask_config.json` at save time (`model.py:111-116`); have `api/fastapi_multitask.py`, `api/fastapi_distilled.py`, `tier2_triple_extractor.py`, and every eval script read it. Delete the literals. Copy the pattern from `species_qa_service/app.py:113-125`, which already does exactly this via `qa_bert/results/qa_eval.json`'s `best_tau`.
**Impact:** high. **Effort:** hours.
