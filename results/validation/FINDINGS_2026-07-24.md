# Validation experiments — 2026-07-24

Two quick-win experiments run as part of the thesis refinement (see
`MetaP/thesis_docs/refinement_plan.md`). Both are CPU-only, local, reproducible.

## 1. Coherence score: sqrt vs calibrated hypergeometric (intrinsic H4a)

`validate_reranking_globi.py` now threads `--coherence {sqrt,hypergeom}` and
`--population N` through to `rerank_sample` (default `sqrt`, backward-compatible).
500 GloBI communities, seed 42, β=1. GloBI universe = **513,335 species**.

| coherence | population N | ΔMRR (CI95) | ΔNDCG (CI95) | mean Δrank | partners↑ | perm p |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|--------------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| sqrt (published) | — | +0.033 [0.023, 0.044] | +0.028 [0.023, 0.035] | 0.45 | 33% | <0.02 |
| hypergeom | 100 | +0.005 [−0.003, 0.013] | +0.007 | 0.10 | 12.5% | — |
| hypergeom | 1,000 | +0.038 [0.026, 0.051] | +0.036 | 0.58 | 33% | — |
| **hypergeom** | **513,335 (all GloBI)** | **+0.203 [0.181, 0.227]** | **+0.218 [0.205, 0.229]** | **3.38** | **88%** | **<0.001** |

Output files: `rerank_sqrt.json`, `rerank_hypergeom.json`,
`rerank_hypergeom_pop{100,1000}.json`.

**Honest interpretation (for the thesis — do NOT report "6× better" naively):**
- The hypergeometric score gives coherence a probabilistic meaning (upper-tail
  surprise −log10 P(X≥k) under a random-community null) and is **parameter-free**
  (no √ heuristic).
- Its advantage over sqrt is **strongly dependent on the assumed universe size N**:
  negligible/worse at small N, comparable to sqrt at N≈1,000, and large at the
  principled N (the full pool of 513,335 GloBI taxa from which the simulated
  communities are actually drawn). The surprise magnitude grows with N, so at
  fixed β the coherence term dominates the read term more as N grows.
- **Correct claim:** under the principled universe (the taxon pool detections are
  drawn from), the calibrated score substantially out-recovers documented partners
  vs the √ heuristic, AND it is degree-corrected — so the intrinsic gain is *not* a
  generalist-hub artifact (it grows, not shrinks, under proper degree correction).
- **Methodological note for H4b:** on real datasets, N must be set to the
  reference-database candidate-taxon pool, not all of GloBI. Report the N-sensitivity.

## 2. Retraction scan of the KG evidence base (H5 go/no-go gate)

Intersected the 4,927 unique DOIs backing GloBI interaction edges
(`globi_trust_index.csv`) against the full Retraction Watch database
(62,288 records with an OriginalPaperDOI, downloaded 2026-07-24).

**Result: 4 retracted/EoC papers back 5 KG edges** (0.08% of DOIs) — all host–pathogen:

| Edge | Interaction | Retracted DOI | Nature |
|------|-------------|---------------|--------|
| *Cryptococcus neoformans* → *Mus musculus* | pathogenOf | 10.1093/femsyr/foaa015 | Retraction |
| *Burkholderia pseudomallei* → *Homo sapiens* | pathogenOf | 10.1371/journal.pone.0096298 | Retraction |
| *Burkholderia pseudomallei* → *Mus musculus* | pathogenOf | 10.1371/journal.pone.0096298 | Retraction |
| *Xanthomonas oryzae* → *Oryza sativa* | pathogenOf | 10.1371/journal.pone.0093205 | Expr. of concern |
| *Xanthomonas oryzae* → *Oryza sativa* | pathogenOf | 10.1126/science.1173438 | Retraction |

**Gate verdict:** real retracted ecological-interaction edges EXIST but are RARE
(as the critics predicted). Therefore H5 is framed as **mechanism + does-no-harm +
auditability + a real named case study**, NOT a powered accuracy/prevalence claim.
The retraction gate `w_edge=0 for ρ=0` correctly zeroes these 5 real edges — a
concrete demonstration on actual data. *Oryza sativa* is a detected taxon in the
honey rbcL dataset, but the retracted edge's partner (*Xanthomonas oryzae*, a
bacterium) is not co-detected under a plant marker, so no headline ranking moves —
consistent with the does-no-harm framing.
