# Instructions for Inserting Figures into LaTeX Manuscript

## In the Methods section, after "Negative Examples: A Strategic Approach"

Add after the paragraph describing negative sampling:

```latex
\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/figure1_dataset_composition.png}
\caption{Dataset composition showing (A) balanced distribution of positive and negative examples and (B) strategic breakdown of negative examples designed to reduce common false positive patterns.}
\label{fig:dataset}
\end{figure}
```

## In the Results section, after Table 1

```latex
\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{figures/figure2_performance_comparison.png}
\caption{Cross-validation performance comparison across transformer models. BioBERT and BiomedBERT show superior performance, with BiomedBERT achieving the highest F1-score.}
\label{fig:performance}
\end{figure}
```

## In the Results section, "Impact of Diverse Negative Examples"

```latex
\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.85\textwidth]{figures/figure3_false_positive_reduction.png}
\caption{Impact of strategic negative sampling on false positive rates. The diverse negative sampling approach reduces false positives by 60-70\% across all categories compared to random negative sampling.}
\label{fig:fp_reduction}
\end{figure}
```

## In the Results section or Supplementary

```latex
\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.75\textwidth]{figures/figure4_precision_recall.png}
\caption{Precision-Recall curves for all evaluated models, showing the trade-off between precision and recall at different classification thresholds.}
\label{fig:pr_curves}
\end{figure}
```
